## Selective Sweeps

Modeling hard sweep:
1. Choose candidate target haplosomes. 
   Ex: p1.haplosomes, sim.subpopulations.haplosomes
2. choose target haplosome. 
   sample()
3. introduce sweep mutation
   addNewMutation() or addNewDrawnMutation()
   Parameters: (type of mutation, position of mutation on haplosome)
